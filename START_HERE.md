## 💡 Learning Tips

### Daily Routine (Recommended)
- **Morning (1-1.5 hours)**: Theory reading
- **Afternoon/Evening (2-2.5 hours)**: Hands-on coding
- **Weekend**: Projects and review

### Best Practices
1. ✅ **Code Every Day**: Even 30 minutes helps
2. ✅ **Build Projects**: Don't just read, code!
3. ✅ **Take Notes**: Document what you learn
4. ✅ **Ask Questions**: Join communities
5. ✅ **Review Regularly**: Reinforce learning
6. ✅ **Be Patient**: Mastery takes time

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. ❌ Skipping fundamentals
2. ❌ Just reading, not coding
3. ❌ Moving too fast
4. ❌ Not practicing enough
5. ❌ Giving up when stuck
